Our Core Process

Chemical Etching Specialists

At the heart of everything we build is chemical etching — a precision manufacturing process that uses controlled chemical reactions to selectively remove metal, creating permanent markings with extraordinary detail and durability.

With nearly 80 years of etching expertise, TNC has refined this process to an art. Our chemical etching capabilities are why the most demanding industries trust us with their identification and labeling needs.

Learn About Our Process

Chemical etching is our specialty — but we also offer screen printing, laser engraving, CNC & laser cutting, digital printing, punching & forming, and finishing. Explore all capabilities →

Precision

Tolerances as tight as ±0.001” with intricate detail reproduction impossible through stamping or engraving.

Durability

Chemically etched markings won’t wear, fade, or peel — even in the harshest industrial environments.

Versatility

Works across stainless steel, aluminum, brass, and specialty alloys with equal precision.

No Tooling Costs

Unlike stamping or die-cutting, chemical etching requires no hard tooling — reducing setup cost and lead time.
